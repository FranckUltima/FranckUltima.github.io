What Kumo does

Kumo detects faces in the photos and videos you choose and covers them (cloud, blur, pixels, sticker or 3D mask) before you save or share them. Detection and covering happen automatically, on your device.

Automatic detection is not perfect

Like any automatic tool, Kumo can make mistakes. In particular, it may miss a face or cover it only partly, for example when a face is very small, far away, in profile, partly hidden, blurred by motion, poorly lit, or appears only briefly in a video. In a video, a covering may also lose a face for a moment.

Check the result before you share it

You are responsible for checking the result before saving, sharing or publishing it: look at the whole photo, and play the whole video, to make sure every face you want hidden is covered. If a face was missed, use Add a face to cover it, or do not share the file.
